AMG Agenda

A class scheduler for golf coaching. Plan a class once, then keep it in your calendar, your task list and your records without retyping anything.

What it does

  • Plans recurring classes with the students attending each one.
  • Creates matching events in Google Calendar or an iCloud calendar, and keeps them in sync both ways.
  • Creates matching tasks in Google Tasks or Apple Reminders.
  • Exports class records as rows in a Google Sheet, one row per class.
  • Keeps event titles in the form the coach uses — class type plus student names — and event notes in the coach's own template.

How it connects to Google

You sign in with your own Google account and grant access to your calendar and your task lists. AMG Agenda then creates, updates and deletes only the events and tasks it created itself. It never reads your Gmail, your Drive files or your contacts, and it never sells or shares your data. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Who builds it

AMG Agenda is built and operated by AMG Golf Performance, a golf coaching business in Spain. It began as the coach's own scheduling tool and runs on the operator's own server.
